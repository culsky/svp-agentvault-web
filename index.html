Execution Architecture

Intelligence without unrestricted authority.

AI generates structured proposals. Independent deterministic controls decide whether those proposals may progress toward execution.

01 · Gemini AI Generates a structured treasury proposal.
02 · Validation Deterministic input and risk checks.
03 · Policy Gate Live AgentVault policy preflight.
04 · Authentication Sealed queue and EIP-712 agent authentication.
05 · SVPChain Approved transaction executes on-chain.
Core Principles

Built around explicit security boundaries.

01 / UNTRUSTED AI

AI proposes only

Model output is treated as untrusted input. AI does not receive owner or guardian authority.

02 / DETERMINISTIC POLICY

Rules are enforceable

Transaction limits, daily limits, allowlists and replay protection are enforced independently of AI reasoning.

03 / AUTHORITY SEPARATION

Roles stay separated

Owner, agent and guardian roles provide explicit operational boundaries instead of one unrestricted execution key.

Authority Boundary

The AI is useful precisely because it is not trusted.

AgentVault separates intelligence from authority. Model output can influence proposals, but cannot override deterministic policy or obtain privileged treasury control.

✓ AI CAN
✓ Generate structured treasury proposals
✓ Evaluate contextual information
✓ Recommend targets, values and actions
✓ Submit proposals into deterministic validation
× AI CANNOT
× Access owner or guardian private keys
× Override AgentVault policy
× Bypass nonce or replay protection
× Independently authorize the current execution flow
